# Research questions

1. What creates profitable entrepreneurial opportunities?
2. How do learning, entry and investment delays affect adjustment?
3. How do market structure, costs, innovation and barriers change long-run outcomes?
4. How do institutions and collective decisions shape opportunities and distribute benefits?
5. Can learners apply these ideas to a business outside the example?

Questions 1–3 motivate the core lessons and simulations. Question 4 informs the political economy chapter. Question 5 is open. This project uses synthesized examples for instruction and does not report empirical calibration or a systematic review.

US Census [Business Dynamics Statistics](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/bds.html) and BLS [Business Employment Dynamics](https://www.bls.gov/bdm/) may support later empirical work about firm and establishment changes. They do not directly measure local awareness, owner opportunity costs or the full economic profit needed here.
